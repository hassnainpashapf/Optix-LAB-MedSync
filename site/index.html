FAQ

Questions, Answered

Is my data safe?

Your lab’s data is stored on our cloud server, and every lab’s data is fully isolated — other labs can never see your patients or reports. Only the staff accounts you create can sign in, and we back up the server.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The Windows and Mac desktop app keeps working when your internet is down and syncs automatically with the cloud once you are back online. The web app needs an internet connection.

Can I use it on multiple PCs?

Yes. Sign in with your lab account on several PCs, on Android or on the web, and everything stays in sync through the cloud. Your plan sets how many staff users you can add — see the plans above.

What happens after the trial?

When the 14-day trial ends, your account becomes read-only until a plan is activated. Nothing is deleted — once your plan is active you continue exactly where you left off.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export your lab’s data as a backup from Settings at any time. Your data belongs to you.

Which payment methods do you accept?

JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer. No credit card is needed to start your trial. After you pay, submit your transaction ID and your plan is activated within a few hours.
